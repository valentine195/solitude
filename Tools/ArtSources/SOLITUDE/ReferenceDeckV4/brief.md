# Reference-led flooring pass

User direction: use the supplied SpaceShip sheets as inspiration while delivering a true pixel grid. Earlier studies were too beige, too featureless, or too repetitive.

References: Assets/_Project/Art/Imported/SpaceShip/1.png through 5.png. Most direct flooring references are 1.png and 5.png: inset gray steel plates, graphite frames, cool metal highlights, vents, mesh, utility channels, and selective amber paint. Reference originals are preserved untouched.

Scope: a coherent floor asset family and review preview, outside Unity Assets. Keep the image generation source, native-size exports, nearest-neighbor previews, and a separate Blender texture-preview document. Any Blender document from this pass is a textured source preview, not a reconstruction of the individual metallic details as 3D geometry.

Production intent: 64x64-pixel modules covering 2x2 of SOLITUDE's native 32x32 cells. Floor surfaces are opaque; slices and enlargements must use exact integer coordinates. Verify dimensions, alpha, palette, reassembly, and repetition. A true raster pixel lattice alone does not prove visual quality or arbitrary edge compatibility.
