Root locations: the enabled build scene is recorded in
`ProjectSettings/EditorBuildSettings.asset` (currently
`Assets/_Project/Scenes/Wakeup.unity`). Other scenes are under
`Assets/_Project/Scenes/` and feature/art folders. Unity version and packages are
recorded in `ProjectSettings/ProjectVersion.txt`, `Packages/manifest.json`, and
`Packages/packages-lock.json`. Verification tooling lives in `Tools/Verification/`.

Assembly boundaries: `Domain/SOLITUDE.Domain.asmdef` has no Unity references;
`Application/SOLITUDE.Application.asmdef` references Domain and has no Unity
references. `SOLITUDE.Runtime.asmdef` references both plus Unity packages.
`Editor/SOLITUDE.Editor.asmdef` and the test `.asmdef` files define their own
boundaries; inspect `.asmref` files when ownership is unclear.

Search first-party owning directories first. Generated `Library/`, `Temp/`,
`Logs/`, builds, `work/` exports, and vendor roots are not default research
targets; inspect them only when needed to answer the question. Exclude every
`.qrspi/` tree from searches and enumeration. Authoring assets, scenes, prefabs,
and metadata can be authoritative wiring evidence even when generated by tools.
