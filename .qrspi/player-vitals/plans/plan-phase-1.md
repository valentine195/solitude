## Phase 1: Hunger and recovery in Wakeup

### Dependencies

- Depends on: none
- Why: This phase extends the current continuous vitals, existing gameplay policy/composition, and current Wakeup HUD. It requires no output from Phase 2; food restoration is checked through the system boundary before actual hotbar use is added.
- Outcome: Wakeup has bounded hunger, eligible delayed recovery within configurable segments, and correct readable HUD markers/status. Consumable activation remains Phase 2 work.
- Inputs: the exact Spec/Research/Approach revisions recorded in `../plan.md`.
