# Repair variation

> “I want to focus on making the game fun.”

The current scrubber interaction feels unsatisfying. Clicking with a wrench feels too thin, and a separate minigame for each machine would get old. Keep the repair itself short and make the satisfying part figuring out what failed, choosing how to deal with it, and seeing what that choice changes aboard the ship.

Explore whether the same equipment can feel different on a new run. Use the Engineer and Technician scrubber playtest to judge whether changing the fault or the surrounding ship conditions changes which repair a player wants to make.

> “In another, the scrubber has lost power. You can restore the local breaker across SEC-0’s patrol route or borrow power from the corridor lights and take the darker route back.”

Keep the Electrical skill meaningful by letting characters with the required proficiency jump circuits. Build on shared repair and ship-system rules so the idea can apply to more equipment later.
