### What existing room, floor, wall, and door authoring behavior is present, and which parts already separate authored layout from generated visuals?

`ShipRoomAuthoring` serializes identity, style, an occupancy Tilemap, a separately owned generated-output root, and a diagnostic. Creation makes a unit Grid with an inactive `EditorOnly` “Authored Occupancy” child and a “Generated Architecture” child. Painting changes occupancy and calls rebuild; the writer clears only the generated root, creates a floor Tilemap, and adds individual wall/corner SpriteRenderers. The floor is therefore generated, rather than the current authoring input. Runtime/save/build lifecycle code deactivates or strips the mask. Sources: `Assets/_Project/Features/World/RoomAuthoring/ShipRoomAuthoring.cs:11`, `Assets/_Project/Features/World/RoomAuthoring/Editor/RoomAuthoringCreation.cs:21`, `Assets/_Project/Features/World/RoomAuthoring/Editor/RoomAuthoringOperations.cs:91`, `Assets/_Project/Features/World/RoomAuthoring/Editor/RoomGeometryWriter.cs:10`, `Assets/_Project/Features/World/RoomAuthoring/Editor/RoomAuthoringEditLifecycle.cs:15`.

Geometry extraction deduplicates and orders cells, extracts cardinal exposed runs and convex corners, and reports whether the occupied cell count fills its bounding rectangle. Placement currently accepts an empty room or one filled rectangle; it repeats modules along runs after start/end reservations and places four corner modules. Sources: `Assets/_Project/Features/World/RoomAuthoring/Geometry/RoomBoundaryExtractor.cs:10`, `Assets/_Project/Features/World/RoomAuthoring/Generation/RoomPlacementBuilder.cs:24`.

The older starting-room builder creates floor, north-wall, and perimeter Tilemaps from a fixed 10×9 array; its floor interior is 8×6. It builds separate colliders and a sliding-door object, leaving a door opening out of the wall Tilemap. Its rectangle-boundary RuleTile uses explicit interior bounds and cardinal neighbors. Sources: `Assets/_Project/Features/World/SolitudeStart/Editor/SolitudeStartBuilder.cs:66`, `Assets/_Project/Features/World/SolitudeStart/Editor/SolitudeStartBuilder.cs:132`, `Assets/_Project/Features/World/SolitudeStart/SolitudeBoundaryRuleTile.cs:11`.

Door behavior remains separate: `SolitudeSlidingDoor` serializes visual, nine frames, blocker, frame duration, and optional restoration gate; it advances animation and disables its blocker on completion. Generic `Door` serializes sprite/blocker/open/locked fields; the shown opening branch returns a message without changing those fields, while closing calls `SetOpened(false)`. Sources: `Assets/_Project/Features/World/SolitudeStart/SolitudeSlidingDoor.cs:10`, `Assets/_Project/Features/Interactables/Doors/Door.cs:10`.

### How do existing editor tools record batched changes, restore generated scene content through Undo and Redo, mark scenes dirty, and preserve prefab or scene serialization?

RoomEdit starts an Undo group, names it, then flushes and collapses it on disposal. Each cell sample records a complete occupancy snapshot before SetTile; a box records once before SetTilesBlock and rebuilds once. Rebuild uses Undo-created and Undo-destroyed generated objects; room diagnostic changes use RecordObject/SetDirty, and the room's scene is marked dirty.
