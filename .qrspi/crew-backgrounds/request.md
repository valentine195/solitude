# Crew backgrounds

“Create a request.md to make 2 backgrounds”

Start with the **Engineer** and **Technician** from the eight-background crew roster. Both should be playable in the same short scrubber encounter. The point is to see whether a colonist’s background changes how the player reads a fault and decides what to do about it.

## Starting character stats

Use the same starting Health, Oxygen, and Hunger for both backgrounds. Use the same base movement speed, carry capacity, and weapon damage.

Use three proficiency ranks for this trial. **Basic (0)** covers ordinary interactions and standard repairs. **Trained (1)** adds diagnostic information and routine actions in the field. **Specialist (2)** adds a distinct way to solve a problem.

| Background | Specialist (2) | Trained (1) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engineer | Electrical | Field Repair |
| Technician | Field Repair | Electrical |

## First playtest

Play the same maintenance room as each background, with two authored scrubber faults.

- **Loss of power.** The normal breaker is across SEC-0’s patrol route. Either background can reach it and restore the normal feed. The Engineer can also use a reusable jumper at the service panel to run the scrubber from the corridor lighting circuit. Show that choice and its cost before the player commits. The scrubber starts, the corridor goes dark, and the jumper stays in the panel while the bypass is in use.
- **Broken intake linkage.** Either background can fit the correct replacement part. The Technician can also rig the linkage with salvaged hardware and get airflow running without making the trip for the part.

Keep the tool action short and physical. Let players read the fault from the room, choose a route or workaround, and see and hear the result. Once airflow recovers, the return door the player saw shut on the way in opens.

This first slice covers two backgrounds, two authored faults, and one room. The other six backgrounds, skill progression, and procedural fault selection can follow once the playtest shows whether these abilities lead to different decisions.

## Clarifications

- Use two separate fault setups for the same room. The return door opens after airflow recovers in either setup.
- With Trained Electrical, the Technician can trace the dead feed to the breaker. With Trained Field Repair, the Engineer can inspect the intake linkage and identify the replacement part. Either background can still perform the standard repair.
- Use the existing shared starting vitals for both backgrounds so the playtest isolates what their skills change.
- Playtesters can launch each background-and-fault combination separately. A background or fault selection screen is outside this playtest scope.
