# QRSPI Explain: crew-backgrounds — phase-1-step-5

## What this change does

The playtest puts the Engineer and Technician in the same maintenance room with the same starting stats and gives each a different way to get the scrubber running. Both can always take the standard route for the active fault, and each has a specialist option that changes what the player needs to fetch or where they need to go.

## Background

The earlier scrubber demo starts with one blown fuse and a restart. Here, the four entries under **SOLITUDE → Demos → Crew Backgrounds** load one editable Tilemap scene. Each entry picks a background and one fault. The scene sets up a fresh disposable session with the shared avatar, the authored SEC-0 patrol, the supplies for that fault, and a closed return door. The Editor hands the preset to the room once, so ordinary Play can use the scene's own defaults. ## Walkthrough

**The background changes what the player knows and can do.** The Engineer has Specialist Electrical and Trained Field Repair. The Technician has Trained Electrical and Specialist Field Repair. On the first interaction, the control uses the relevant skill to choose a clue. Both backgrounds can trace the dead feed to the main breaker and identify the replacement intake linkage. On the next interaction, the control checks the action's minimum rank and whether the equipment and inventory can support it. Basic is enough for standard repair. The specialist options ask for rank 2 and show the requirement when the player's rank falls short. [Background ranks](../../../Assets/_Project/Features/World/CrewBackgrounds/CrewBackgroundsPlaytestSetup.cs#L27) · [Inspection and action checks](../../../Assets/_Project/Features/World/CrewBackgrounds/CrewBackgroundsRepairControl.cs#L123)

| Scrubber fault | Standard route for either background | Specialist option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loss of Power | Cross SEC-0's patrol route and reset the main breaker. The scrubber gets normal power and the corridor stays lit. | The Engineer can bring a reusable jumper to the nearby service panel. Inspection warns that bypass power will start the scrubber and turn off the corridor lights. Completing the action moves the jumper from the player's inventory into the panel. |
| Broken Intake Linkage | Bring a replacement intake linkage to the scrubber and fit it. **All four routes use the same short repair sequence.** Inspection shows the clue or tradeoff. Starting the job animates the control's indicator. Moving away or canceling before completion leaves the equipment and the materials as they were. When the work finishes, the control checks the selected action, required rank, equipment state, and materials again. For either linkage route, the equipment marks the linkage intact as part of the command that consumes one configured item. A repeated attempt finds the linkage already repaired. [Repair completion](../../../Assets/_Project/Features/World/CrewBackgrounds/CrewBackgroundsRepairControl.cs#L151) · [Work feedback and interruption](../../../Assets/_Project/Features/World/CrewBackgrounds/CrewBackgroundsRepairControl.cs#L240) · [Linkage and breaker operations](../../../Assets/_Project/Domain/Restoration/CrewBackgroundsEncounterState.cs#L48)

**The reusable jumper gives the bypass a life after the repair.** The panel owns one inventory slot reserved for the jumper. With the normal feed down, the presence of the jumper in that slot supplies bypass power and turns off the corridor lights. Either background can reset the breaker while bypass is running. The lights come back, airflow continues, and recovery keeps its progress. The panel still holds the inactive jumper, and the player can retrieve that same item when there is room to carry it. [Panel slot](../../../Assets/_Project/Features/World/CrewBackgrounds/CrewBackgroundsServicePanel.cs#L24) · [Power and airflow](../../../Assets/_Project/Domain/Restoration/CrewBackgroundsEncounterState.cs#L18) · [Jumper transfer](../../../Assets/_Project/Domain/Restoration/CrewBackgroundsEncounterState.cs#L84)

**Every successful route opens the same way out.** The repaired scrubber changes its indicator and sound, and the room reports that air is recovering. Once it has had five seconds of airflow during active gameplay, the return door starts its usual opening animation. The blocker clears at the end of that animation. The checkpoint also gives the shared controller button one action per context, so a press that interacts in exploration can serve as Cancel when a menu is open. ## Where to focus

The rig bracket shows how a future crew background can change play on equipment that is already in the room. The scene gives both linkage actions the same repair operation. Each action supplies its own access point, clue, required rank, and material. Another background can qualify for those options through its skill ranks, and another machine with a similar repair can use the same control with its own equipment state and part. The second-target test sets up exactly that reuse. It configures a different required item and checks that repairing the second target leaves the room scrubber and its replacement part untouched. A new effect, such as diverting power, still needs an equipment operation that defines what happens when the action succeeds. [Action settings](../../../Assets/_Project/Features/World/CrewBackgrounds/CrewBackgroundsRepairControl.cs#L15) · [Rig and replacement bindings](../../../Assets/_Project/Scenes/Demos/CrewBackgroundsMaintenance.unity#L10980) · [Second-target scenario](../../../Assets/_Project/Tests/PlayMode/CrewBackgroundsRepairJourneyTests.cs#L191)

The point to watch when playing is the handoff from bypass to normal power. The same breaker action can restore airflow from a cold start or keep airflow running during a power change. In the latter case, the corridor relights immediately from the new supply, the recovery clock continues, and retrieving the jumper is a separate interaction.
